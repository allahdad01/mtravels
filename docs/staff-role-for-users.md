# Staff Role — Guide for Clients

## What is the Staff Role?

The **Staff** role is for **regular employees** who only need to:
- **Check in and out** for attendance
- **View their salary payments**
- **Set up two-factor authentication** (2FA)
- **View tutorials**

That's it. The Staff role is intentionally minimal — no access to bookings, finances, clients, or administration.

---

## What Can the Staff Role Do?

### 1. Staff Dashboard
When you log in, you see a **simple, clean dashboard** showing:
- **Your name** and a greeting
- **This month's attendance summary** — how many days you were present, absent, or on leave
- **Your attendance rate** (percentage)
- **Your recent salary payments** — last 5 payments with dates and amounts
- **Total paid this month**

### 2. My Attendance
| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Check In** | Mark yourself present at the start of your shift |
| **Check Out** | Mark yourself leaving at the end of your shift |
| **View History** | See your attendance record |

### 3. My Payments
| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **View Payments** | See your salary payment history |
| **Payment Details** | Amount, date, description, and status of each payment |

### 4. Account Security
| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **2FA** | Set up two-factor authentication for extra security |

### 5. Training
| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Tutorials** | View training materials and guides |

---

## What the Staff Role CANNOT Do

**Everything else is restricted:**

- ❌ **No travel bookings** — cannot book tickets, hotels, visas, or Umrah
- ❌ **No financial data** — cannot see accounts, debtors, creditors, expenses, or sarafi
- ❌ **No client or supplier records**
- ❌ **No HR management** — cannot manage other employees or attendance settings
- ❌ **No salary management** — cannot process payments or see others' salaries
- ❌ **No reports** — cannot generate any reports
- ❌ **No communication tools** — no chat, no email analytics
- ❌ **No support tickets** — cannot create or view support requests
- ❌ **No search** — cannot search the system
- ❌ **No activity log** — cannot see system activity
- ❌ **No branch switching** — cannot change branches
- ❌ **Cannot manage anything** — this is a view-only self-service role

---

## How This Role Fits in Your Company

The Staff role is designed for **entry-level employees, drivers, cleaners, or any staff member** who doesn't need system access beyond:
- Tracking their own attendance
- Viewing their own payments
- Keeping their account secure

Everything else is handled by managers, admin, finance, and sales roles.

## Summary

| Can Do | Cannot Do |
|--------|-----------|
| ✅ Check in/out for attendance | ❌ Book travel services |
| ✅ View your salary payments | ❌ See financial data |
| ✅ Set up 2FA security | ❌ Manage anything |
| ✅ View tutorials | ❌ Access admin functions |

It's a simple, focused role for employees who just need to track their work and pay.
