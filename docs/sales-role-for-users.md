# Sales Role — Guide for Clients

## What is the Sales Role?

The **Sales** role is for **travel agents and sales representatives** working in a single branch. You focus on what matters most: booking travel services for customers. You can book tickets, hotels, visas, and umrah packages, manage client records, and generate reports — but you don't deal with finances, HR, or administration.

---

## What Can the Sales Role Do?

### 1. Travel & Services (Full Access)
| Module | What You Can Do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Tickets** | Book tickets, manage reservations, process refunds, change dates, manage weights |
| **Hotels** | Book hotels, process refunds |
| **Umrah** | Book umrah packages, manage services, process refunds and date changes |
| **Visas** | Submit visa applications, process refunds |

### 2. Client Management
| Module | What You Can Do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Clients** | Create new client records, edit, view history |
| **Search** | Find clients, bookings, and records quickly |

### 3. Reports
| Module | What You Can Do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Reports** | Generate reports for tickets, hotels, visas, umrah |

### 4. Standard Access
| Module | What You Can Do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Support Tickets** | Create and manage support requests |
| **Tutorials** | View training materials |
| **2FA** | Set up two-factor authentication |

---

## What the Sales Role CANNOT Do

- ❌ **No financial access** — cannot see accounts, debtors, creditors, or expenses
- ❌ **No sarafi (forex)** — cannot access money exchange
- ❌ **No HR or salary** — cannot manage employees or process payroll
- ❌ **No administrative tasks** — cannot manage users, letters, assets, or settings
- ❌ **No budget management** — cannot allocate budgets
- ❌ **No chat settings** — cannot configure communication
- ❌ See data from other branches

---

## How This Role Fits in Your Company

The Sales role is designed for **frontline staff** who interact with customers and book services. You handle the **revenue-generating activities** while financial and administrative tasks are handled by other roles.

## Summary

The **Sales** role gives you everything you need to serve customers:
- **Full booking capabilities** for all travel services
- **Client management** to track customer relationships
- **Reporting** to analyze your sales performance
- **No distractions** — financial and admin modules are hidden
