# 📦 M.Travels Assets Module — Features

Track and manage every physical and digital asset your company owns — electronics, furniture, vehicles, office equipment, real estate, software, and more. Monitor depreciation, assign assets to employees, track warranties, and see everything in visual charts.

---

## 🏷️ Manage Assets

- **Add an Asset** — Register any company asset with its name, category, purchase date, purchase value, current value, and currency (USD, EUR, AFS, DARHAM, PKR, or INR). Add location, serial number, warranty expiry date, condition, and who it's assigned to.
- **Edit Details** — Update any asset information at any time.
- **View Details** — Click the eye icon to see all asset information, depreciation status, and attached documents in a single modal.
- **Delete an Asset** — Permanently remove an asset record and its uploaded documents.

---

## 🔄 Asset Lifecycle

Every asset can be in one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | In use and operational |
| **Inactive** | Not in use but still owned |
| **Maintenance** | Being repaired or serviced |
| **Sold** | No longer owned |
| **Disposed** | Discarded or written off |

Change status with one click — each change is confirmed before taking effect.

---

## 📉 Depreciation Tracking

The system automatically calculates depreciation as a percentage: the difference between purchase value and current value.

- **Green bar** — Less than 25% depreciated
- **Yellow bar** — 25% to 50% depreciated
- **Orange bar** — 50% to 75% depreciated
- **Red bar** — More than 75% depreciated

See the depreciation bar in both the asset list and the details modal.

---

## 📎 Document Attachments

Upload supporting documents for any asset — receipts, warranty cards, photos, manuals. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, XLS, XLSX (up to 10MB). View or download documents directly from the details modal.

---

## 📊 Visual Analytics

Two interactive charts give you a bird's-eye view of your assets:

- **Category Distribution** (Doughnut chart) — See how assets are spread across categories
- **Status Distribution** (Bar chart) — See how many assets are in each status

---

## 🔍 Smart Filtering

Find any asset quickly with:

- **Status Tabs** — Switch between All, Active, Maintenance, Inactive, Sold, Disposed
- **Category Filter** — Narrow by asset category
- **Location Search** — Find assets by physical location
- **Date Range** — Filter by purchase date
- **Condition Filter** — New, Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor
- **Free-Text Search** — Search by name, serial number, or category

---

## 📋 KPI Dashboard Cards
