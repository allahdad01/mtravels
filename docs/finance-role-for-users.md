# Finance Role — Guide for Clients

## What is the Finance Role?

The **Finance** role is for **accountants and financial staff** working in a single branch. You can see and manage all financial data — accounts, debtors, creditors, expenses, salaries — but you **cannot permanently delete records**. This is a safety measure to prevent accidental data loss.

You also get a special **Finance Wallet** tool that no other role has.

---

## What Can the Finance Role Do?

### 1. Finance Dashboard
You see the standard dashboard with KPIs, charts, and alerts. You **cannot** see the admin-only sections (wealth distribution, performance overview, top performers).

### 2. Financial Management
| Module | What You Can Do | What You CANNOT Do |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Accounts** | View account balances, see all accounts | Cannot manage main accounts or delete things |
| **Debtors** | View debtors, add payments, print receipts | ❌ Cannot delete debtors or transactions |
| **Creditors** | View creditors, add payments, print receipts | ❌ Cannot delete creditors or transactions |
| **Sarafi (Forex)** | Full control — record exchanges, deposits, withdrawals | — |
| **Expenses** | Full control — record and manage expenses | — |
| **Budget Allocation** | Full control — allocate budgets | — |
| **Additional Payments** | Full control — manage extra payments | — |
| **JV Payments** | Full control — process joint voucher payments | — |
| **Reports** | Full access — generate all report types | — |

### 3. HR & Salary
| Module | What You Can Do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Salary Management** | Process payments, view history |
| **Employee Management** | View employee records |

### 4. Unique Feature: Finance Wallet
You have access to a special **Finance Wallet** page (Finance Tracker) that helps monitor financial metrics. No other role can see this page.

### 5. Standard Modules
- **Clients** — view and manage
- **Suppliers** — view and manage
- **Search** — search across the system
- **Activity Log** — view system activity
- **Support Tickets** — create and manage

---

## What the Finance Role CANNOT Do

- ❌ **Delete anything** (debtors, creditors, transactions, accounts)
- ❌ Manage users (add/edit/delete staff accounts)
- ❌ See admin-only dashboard widgets (wealth distribution, performance)
- ❌ Generate quarterly tax reports
- ❌ See data from other branches
- ❌ Manage HR settings or attendance settings
- ❌ Manage letters, assets, or chat settings

---

## Summary

The **Finance** role is perfect for accountants who need:
- **Full visibility** into all financial data
- **Operational control** to create transactions and process payments
- **Safety** — no ability to permanently delete records
- **A dedicated Finance Wallet** for financial tracking

This role balances power with protection — you can do your job without risk of accidentally deleting critical financial records.
