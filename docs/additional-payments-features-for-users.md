# 💰 M.Travels Additional Payments Module — Features

Record and track any non-standard income your agency receives — service fees, commissions, miscellaneous payments, or any revenue that doesn't fit into your main service categories. Track profits, link payments to suppliers or clients, and see everything in your dashboard reports.

---

## 💳 Manage Additional Payments

- **Add a Payment** — Record any payment with a type/category, description, base amount, sold amount, and automatically calculated profit. Choose the currency (USD, AFS, EUR, or DARHAM) and link it to your main account.
- **Edit a Payment** — Update the details anytime. If linked to a supplier or client, their balances are re-synced automatically.
- **Delete a Payment** — Remove a payment (if no transactions exist for it). All linked balances are reversed.

---

## 📊 Payment Status Tracking

Each payment card shows its current status at a glance:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Unpaid** | No payments received yet |
| **Partial** | Some payments received, but not fully paid |
| **Paid** | Fully paid |
| **Overpaid** | Received more than the sold amount |

---

## 💵 Manage Transactions (Payment Installments)

- **Add a Transaction** — Record a payment received against an additional payment. Choose the currency and amount, and the money goes into your main account.
- **Edit a Transaction** — Update the amount, description, or receipt number.
- **Delete a Transaction** — Remove a transaction and reverse the main account balance.
- **Multi-Currency Support** — Each transaction can be in a different currency with automatic exchange rate conversion.
- **Print Receipt** — Print a professional receipt for any transaction.

---

## 🔗 Link to Suppliers & Clients

- **Supplier Linkage** — When a payment comes from a supplier, their outstanding balance is reduced automatically.
- **Client Linkage** — When a payment is for a client, their outstanding balance is reduced automatically.
- **Combined View** — The detail page shows transactions from your main account, supplier, and client accounts all in one place.

---

## 👁️ Client Portal

- **Client List Page** — Clients can see their own additional payments with summary cards (total count, USD total, AFS total, grand total) and filter by currency.
- **Client Detail Page** — Clients can view payment details and their own transactions only.

---

## 📈 Dashboard & Reports

- **Dashboard Due Card** — See USD and AFS dues with a progress bar right on your main dashboard.
- **Profit Calculations** — Additional payments are included in daily, weekly, and yearly profit reports.
- **Sales Reports** — Included in filtered sales data aggregated by day, month, or year.
- **Expense Reports** — Included as an income source in expense management charts and comprehensive reports.
- **Export** — Download additional payment reports as PDF, Excel, or Word.

---

## 🔍 Super Admin Overview

- **Cross-Branch Dashboard** — View additional payments across all branches with stats for total payments, USD total, AFS total, and profit in both currencies.
- **Branch Filter** — Focus on payments from a specific branch.
