# Umrah Staff Role — Guide for Clients

## What is the Umrah Role?

The **Umrah** role is a **specialized role for staff who handle only Umrah operations**. If your job is focused entirely on managing Umrah packages — bookings, services, refunds, and date changes — this is the role for you. The system is tailored so you only see what's relevant to Umrah.

---

## What Can the Umrah Role Do?

### 1. Umrah Operations (Full Control)
| Module | What You Can Do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Umrah Bookings** | Create and manage Umrah package bookings |
| **Umrah Services** | Manage additional services for Umrah pilgrims |
| **Umrah Refunds** | Process refunds for cancelled Umrah packages |
| **Umrah Date Changes** | Change dates for existing Umrah bookings |

### 2. Client Management
| Module | What You Can Do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Clients** | Create and manage client records |
| **Search** | Search for clients and bookings |

### 3. Umrah-Only Reports
When you generate reports, the system **only shows Umrah-related options**:
- Umrah Bookings Report
- Umrah Refunds Report

Other report types (tickets, hotels, visas) are hidden because they're not relevant to your role.

### 4. Standard Access
| Module | What You Can Do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Support Tickets** | Create and manage support requests |
| **Tutorials** | View training materials |
| **2FA** | Set up two-factor authentication |

---

## What the Umrah Role CANNOT Do

- ❌ **No tickets** — cannot book or manage airline tickets
- ❌ **No hotels** — cannot book or manage hotel reservations
- ❌ **No visas** — cannot process visa applications
- ❌ **No financial access** — cannot see accounts, debtors, creditors, or expenses
- ❌ **No HR or salary** — cannot manage employees or payroll
- ❌ **No administrative tasks** — cannot manage users or settings
- ❌ **Generate non-Umrah reports** — report options are limited to Umrah
- ❌ See data from other branches

---

## How This Role Fits in Your Company

The Umrah role is designed for **specialized Umrah staff** who handle the entire Umrah workflow for pilgrims. Since Umrah involves unique processes (packages, services, group management), this role keeps the interface clean and focused.

## Summary

The **Umrah** role is perfect for staff who:
- Handle **only Umrah bookings and services**
- Don't need access to tickets, hotels, or visas
- Want a **clean, focused interface** with Umrah-only reports
- Don't deal with finances, HR, or administration
