# 🕋 M.Travels Umrah Management System — Features

Welcome to the all-in-one Umrah management solution. Here is everything our system can do for you — from registering a family to generating official documents in multiple languages.

---

## 📋 Family Management

- **Register Families** — Add a family by entering the head of family name, contact details, address, province, district, and package type
- **Edit or Remove** — Update family info anytime, or delete a family if needed
- **Smart Search** — Find any family by name, contact number, or member passport number instantly
- **Filter Families** — View families by visa status (Not Applied / Applied / Issued) or by Refunded / Cancelled status
- **Visual Dashboard** — See your total revenue, collected amount, and outstanding balance at a glance
- **Pagination** — Browse through families page by page

---

## 👥 Member (Pilgrim) Management

- **Add Multiple Members at Once** — Register several pilgrims in one go with a single form
- **Per-Pilgrim Details** — For each member: full name, father's name, grandfather's name, family relation, date of birth, gender, passport number & expiry, ID type, travel dates, accommodation type, room type, and price
- **Mahram Information** — Automatically required for female pilgrims
- **Passport Validity Check** — System warns if passport has less than 6 months validity
- **Add Services to Each Member** — Assign services like Ticket, Visa, Hotel, Transport, or a combo. Choose suppliers and see auto-calculated profit
- **Edit or Remove Members** — Update any pilgrim's info or remove them from the family
- **View Full Member Details** — See personal info, photo, travel info, account info, and services in one place
- **Approve Bookings** — One-click approval that automatically creates the necessary financial records

---

## 💰 Payment & Financial Management

- **Record Payments in Multiple Currencies** — US Dollar (USD), Afghan Afghani (AFS), Euro (EUR), UAE Dirham (AED)
- **Automatic Exchange Rate** — Enter the rate and the system calculates everything for you
- **Payment History** — View every transaction: date, description, receipt number, amount
- **Edit or Delete Payments** — Fix mistakes easily
- **Family-Level Payments** — See total price, total paid, and total due for the whole family, with a breakdown per member
- **Refunds** — Process full or partial refunds. System shows original amount and profit, and you just enter the refund amount and reason

---

## 📄 Document Generation (One-Click PDFs)

Generate professional documents instantly in **English, Dari, or Pashto**:

| Document | What It Does |
|----------|-------------|
| **Umrah Agreement** | Official pilgrimage contract for an individual or family |
| **Tazmin (Guarantor) Agreement** | Guarantor commitment form with the guarantor's name |
| **Bank Receipt** | Official payment receipt with bank details (Dari & Pashto) |
| **Umrah Receipt** | Simple payment confirmation |
| **Completion Certificate** | Proof that Umrah services have been delivered (individual or family) |
| **Cancellation Form** | Official cancellation document (individual or family) |
| **Umrah Presidency Letter** | Official letter with family details, visa/ticket amounts, airline, hotel info, and stay duration in Makkah & Madina |
| **Group Ticket** | Combined travel document with flight details, airline, PNR, and member list |
| **ID Cards** | Custom ID cards for pilgrims — choose color, add photo, set validity, add guide contact info |
| **Multi-Ticket Invoice** | Combined invoice for multiple tickets across different families |
| **Document Receipt Form** | Track returned documents: passport, ID, photos, vaccination cert, marriage cert, birth cert, visa form, mahram declaration |

## 📅 Date Change Management

- **Request Date Changes** — Change flight and return dates for any booking
- **Track Requests** — Every request goes through a status: Pending → Approved/Rejected → Completed
- **Full History** — See who approved, when, and any remarks
- **Penalty Tracking** — Record supplier penalties and service penalties

---

## ❌ Cancellation & Re-apply

- **Cancel Bookings** — Mark a booking as cancelled with a reason
- **Re-apply Cancelled Bookings** — Restore a cancelled booking and recalculate the profit automatically
- **Bulk Processing** — Cancel or re-apply multiple bookings at once
- **Document Return Tracking** — When cancelling a family, track which documents have been returned (passport, etc.)

---

## 📸 Passport & Photo OCR (Smart Document Reader)

- **Drag & Drop Upload** — Just drag passport images into the upload area
- **Auto Fill from Passport** — The system reads the passport using OCR and fills in the member's name, passport number, and other details automatically
- **Two-level OCR** — Server-side system first; if that fails, your browser's built-in OCR takes over
- **Auto Photo Extraction** — Automatically cut out the passport photo from the scanned passport image
- **Interactive Crop Tool** — Manually select and crop the photo area with a simple drag-and-click interface

---

## ✈️ Flight Status & Details

- **Live Flight Status** — Each family card shows: ✓ Flight Done, ⚠ Partial, or Pending
- **Flight Details View** — Click to see styled ticket cards with airline, dates, PNR, and passenger list

---

## ⚡ Bulk Operations

- **Update Flight Dates in Bulk** — Select multiple families and apply new flight/return dates to all at once
- **Bulk Cancel/Re-apply** — Process multiple bookings in one go

## 🌐 Multilingual (3 Languages)

- **English** — Default interface
- **Dari (Farsi)** — Full support for Afghan clients
- **Pashto** — Full support for Pashto-speaking clients
- Language can be selected per document at generation time

---

## 🛡️ Security & Access Control

- **User Roles** — Admin, Finance, Sales, and Umrah staff each have appropriate access
- **Data Isolation** — Each branch sees only their own families and bookings
- **CSRF Protection** — All form submissions are protected against cross-site request forgery
- **Session Timeout** — Automatic logout after 30 minutes of inactivity
