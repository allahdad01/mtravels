# 🏨 M.Travels Hotel Management System — Features

Manage hotel and accommodation bookings for your clients — from reservation to checkout, payments, refunds, and invoices. All in one dashboard.

---

## 📋 Hotel Bookings

- **Create Bookings Easily** — A simple multi-section form: guest info (name, title, gender), booking details (order ID, issue date, contact), stay details (check-in/out dates, hotel name, room type), financials (base price, selling price, auto-calculated profit), and additional details (supplier, client, payment account, currency)
- **Edit or Delete** — Update any booking anytime, or remove incorrect entries
- **Smart Search** — Find bookings by order ID, guest name, contact number, or hotel name
- **Filter Tabs** — All / Confirmed / Pending / Cancelled — see exactly what you need
- **Night Counter** — System automatically calculates the number of nights and shows it as a badge
- **Color-Coded Status** — Each booking shows its payment status at a glance: Paid (green), Partial (amber), Unpaid (red), Neutral (blue)
- **View Full Details** — Click any booking to see guest info, stay details, financial summary, and parties involved in one modal

---

## 💰 Payments & Transactions

- **Multi-Currency Support** — USD (US Dollar) and AFS (Afghani) supported
- **Auto Exchange Rate** — When paying in a different currency, just enter the rate and the system calculates everything
- **Transaction History** — Every payment recorded: date, description, receipt number, amount, exchange rate
- **Add / Edit / Delete Payments** — Full control over payment records
- **Print Receipts** — Print professional receipts for any transaction
- **Real-Time Balance** — See paid and remaining amounts update instantly per currency

---

## ↩️ Refunds

- **Full Refund** — Refund the entire amount with one click; profit set to zero, booking marked as refunded
- **Partial Refund** — Refund a portion of the amount; profit recalculated proportionally
- **Automatic Balance Updates** — Client balance is credited; supplier balance is adjusted
- **Refund History** — Every refund recorded with type, amount, reason, and processing status
- **Print Refund Agreement** — Generate a refund agreement document
- **Track Processing** — Mark refunds as financially processed when complete

---

## 📄 Multi-Booking Invoice

- **Combine Multiple Bookings** — Select several bookings and generate one combined invoice
- **Professional Layout** — Includes your agency branding, client info, booking table with stay period and amounts, totals, bank account details (USD & AFS), and signature boxes
- **Ready to Print** — Opens in a print-friendly view
- **Floating Action Button** — Quick access from the bookings page

---

## 🔍 Single Booking Detail View

- **Everything in One Page** — See guest info, booking details, financial info, stay details, and all transaction records (client, supplier, main account) on a single page
- **Quick Actions** — Edit, refund, or delete right from the detail page

---

## 🌐 Client Portal

Your clients get their own view:

- **My Bookings** — See all their hotel bookings with summary cards: total bookings, total spent, total nights, confirmed/pending/cancelled counts
- **Urgency Alerts** — Check-in Today (yellow), Check-in Soon (orange), Completed Stay (gray) — so nothing is missed
- **My Refunds** — View refund history with processed/pending status

---

## 🔔 Notifications

- **Email Confirmations** — Clients automatically receive booking confirmation emails
- **WhatsApp Alerts** — Booking notifications sent via WhatsApp when configured
- **Activity Log** — Every action is logged for audit purposes

---

## 🛡️ Security & Access Control

- **User Roles** — Admin, Finance, and Sales each have appropriate permissions
- **Tenant & Branch Isolation** — Each branch sees only their own data
- **Multi-Currency Support** — USD, AFS (Afghani)
- **Feature Flags** — Hotel module can be enabled/disabled per tenant
