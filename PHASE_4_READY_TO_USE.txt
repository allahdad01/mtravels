WHAT GETS LOGGED AUTOMATICALLY
================================================================================

EVERY MESSAGE SEND:
- Who sent it (user_id)
- Who received it (to_user_id)
- When (timestamp)
- Size (bytes)
- If encrypted (yes/no)
- Encryption key ID
- Status (success/failed)
- IP address
- User agent

EVERY MESSAGE READ:
- Who read it (user_id)
- Which message (message_id)
- When (timestamp)
- Status
- IP address

EVERY BLOCK/UNBLOCK:
- Who blocked (user_id)
- Who was blocked (blocked_user_id)
- When
- Action (block/unblock)
- IP address

EVERY MUTE/UNMUTE:
- Who muted (user_id)
- Who was muted (muted_user_id)
- When
- Action (mute/unmute)
- IP address

EVERY FAILED ATTEMPT:
- Who tried (user_id)
- What they tried to do (action)
- Why it failed (reason)
- When
- IP address
